Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order value on Jan?

There is no minimum order value, but each product has a minimum order quantity (MOQ) set by the warehouse. You can combine multiple products to reach any order size.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 24–48 hours depending on your city and the warehouse's capacity. Same-day delivery is available in select cities.

Can I get a GST invoice?

Yes, Jan provides a full GST invoice for every order. You can download it from your order history or receive it via email/WhatsApp automatically.

What is Jan Business Credit?

Jan Business Credit is a 30-day buy-now-pay-later facility available to verified shop owners. Purchase goods today and pay when you've sold them — no interest for 30 days.

Is Jan available in my city?

Jan is currently available in 120+ cities across India. We are expanding rapidly. Enter your PIN code on checkout to confirm serviceability.
